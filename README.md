# Filmkorn Raw-Scanner
## An affordable 12‑Bit 4K Motion Picture Film Scanner

Another DIY film scanner?  
Yes, but this one is different: the Filmkorn Raw Scanner is an open‑source project that enables digitizing small‑gauge films at breathtaking quality for a very low price. The project began in 2020 after a bet on the German [Filmvorführerforum](https://www.filmvorfuehrer.de/topic/31851-challenge-framescanner-f%C3%BCr-350%E2%82%AC-bauen/), when the infamous but similarly priced “Wolverine” film scanner from China (aka Somikon, aka Winait, aka Kodak, aka Hammacher‑Schlemmer) turned out to be an irredeemable film‑scratching disaster: Is it even possible to build a truly high‑quality scanner for under €500?

Yes, it is. Now anyone can build a scanner that digitizes their films gently and in fantastic quality.
